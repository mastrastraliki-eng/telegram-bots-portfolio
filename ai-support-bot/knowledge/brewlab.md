# Brew Lab Coffee Roasters — support knowledge base

## About
Brew Lab is a specialty coffee roastery in Manchester, UK. We roast every Monday and Thursday and ship fresh beans across the UK and EU. Online shop: brewlab.example. Café: 14 Oldham Street, Manchester, open Mon–Fri 7:30–18:00, Sat–Sun 9:00–17:00.

## Coffee range
- **House Blend** (Brazil + Colombia): chocolate, hazelnut, caramel. Medium roast. Great for espresso and milk drinks. 250 g £9.50, 1 kg £32.
- **Ethiopia Yirgacheffe**: jasmine, lemon, peach. Light roast. Best for filter / V60 / AeroPress. 250 g £12.
- **Kenya Kiambu AA**: blackcurrant, grapefruit, juicy. Light-medium. Filter or espresso. 250 g £12.50.
- **Decaf Colombia** (Swiss Water process, no chemicals): milk chocolate, red apple. 250 g £10.
- Every coffee is available as whole beans or ground for: espresso, moka pot, filter, French press. Choose the grind on the product page.
- Best enjoyed 7–30 days after the roast date printed on the bag.

## Brewing tips
- Espresso: 18 g in, 36 g out, 25–30 s, water 93 °C.
- V60 / filter: 15 g coffee per 250 ml water, 94 °C, 2:30–3:00 total brew time.
- French press: 30 g per 500 ml, coarse grind, 4 minutes, then press gently.
- Store beans in the original bag (it has a valve) away from light; don't refrigerate.
